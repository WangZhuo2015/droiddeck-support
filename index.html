IPHONE + IPAD · ANDROID COMPANION

Your Android.
Within reach.

View and control your Android phone, tablet, or emulator from iPhone or iPad over a local network.

在 iPhone 或 iPad 上，通过局域网查看和控制你的 Android 设备。

Connection guide / 连接指南

A direct connection

Pair with Android Wireless Debugging, or connect to a device already configured for ADB over TCP. Touch controls, Android navigation, text input, explicit clipboard transfer and screenshots keep common tasks close at hand.

支持无线调试配对和已配置的 ADB TCP 连接，可进行触控、Android 导航、文字输入、按需剪贴板传递和截图。

One purchase. Local operation.

Planned as a one-time paid app at US$2.99 in the US, with Apple's equivalent local prices elsewhere. No subscription or additional in-app purchase. Availability is subject to App Store review and regional requirements.

采用一次买断：美国区 US$2.99，其他地区采用 Apple 对应本币价格。无需订阅或额外 App 内购买。上架时间与销售地区以 App Store 审核及地区要求为准。

Requires iOS/iPadOS 17 or later. Wireless Debugging requires Android 11 or later. Legacy ADB TCP must be enabled in advance and used only on trusted networks.
