HELP & CONTACT / 使用帮助

Connect with confidence.

Connect directly to an Android device you own or are authorized to control. DroidDeck does not provide a cloud remote-access service.

Wireless Debugging / 无线调试

  1. Use iOS/iPadOS 17 or later and Android 11 or later.
  2. Put both devices on a network that permits them to reach each other. Allow DroidDeck's Local Network permission.
  3. Enable Developer options and Wireless Debugging on Android.
  4. Use DroidDeck's pairing-code or QR pairing flow, then select the paired device. Accept Android authorization prompts when shown.

将两台设备接入可互相访问的网络，允许本地网络权限，在 Android 上启用开发者选项和无线调试，再通过配对码或二维码配对并连接。

Manual connection / 手动连接

For a device with ADB over TCP already enabled, enter its reachable IP address and port. Pairing and connection ports may differ. Legacy TCP is unencrypted; use it only on trusted networks. Never expose ADB to the public internet.

手动输入已启用 ADB over TCP 的设备地址和端口。配对端口与连接端口可能不同。Legacy TCP 不加密，仅适合可信网络，请勿将 ADB 端口暴露到公网。

Troubleshooting / 排查问题

  • Check Local Network permission and confirm Wireless Debugging remains enabled.
  • Check the Android address and port again; they may change after reconnecting Wi-Fi.
  • Guest networks, firewalls and client isolation may prevent connections.
  • Keep DroidDeck in the foreground. Switching to the background ends the session.
  • Photos access is needed only when saving a screenshot.

请检查权限、无线调试开关、地址与端口，并确认网络未开启设备隔离。连接期间保持 App 在前台；保存截图时才需要照片写入权限。
